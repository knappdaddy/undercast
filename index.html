⚙️ How UnderCast Works

🧮 Two independent edges

UnderCast looks for point totals that sit above their fair value for two separate reasons, and shows each on its own so you can see where the value comes from:

Fair = Book − Inflation − (unpriced Weather)

  • Inflation Score — how far your book's number sits above fair, driven mostly by betting-market signals. This is a pre-weather number.
  • Weather — the points the whole market is still under-pricing for the forecast.

📊 The Inflation Score — market signals weighted heaviest

Recreational money loves overs, so books shade popular totals up. The Inflation Score measures that, leaning hardest on the two signals that are actually measured from the market, not guessed:

Book-vs-sharp and line move dwarf the situational nudges by design — the market tells us more than any heuristic. Because both your book and the sharp book see the same forecast, Book − Sharp cancels out weather, so there's no double-counting with the weather edge.

🌬️ Weather — the part the market misses

The weather model is calibrated on 2010–2024 results. It rewards the two repeatable under signals — rain (0.8 / 1.8 / 2.8 by intensity) and 10–19 mph wind — while flattening extreme wind (20+ mph is already priced in), treating snow as neutral (snowy totals are shaded down and trend over), and trimming cold toward neutral.

Since sharp books already price most weather, UnderCast only adds back the fraction the market is slow on:

unpriced Weather = Weather penalty × 40%

The 40% reflects the evidence that markets — even sharp ones — under-react to rain and moderate wind. That residual is exactly the edge you've been winning on.

🔗 Combining & conviction

A game leans UNDER when Inflation + unpriced Weather clears 1.5 pts. Conviction is market-weighted: your book-vs-sharp gap and line move drive it, with weather as a confirming boost. When the market and the weather both point under, the game is flagged 🔥 Stacked — the highest-confidence spot on the board.
