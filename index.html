⚙️ How UnderCast Works

🧮 The core idea

Sportsbooks set a point total (over/under) for each game. Our thesis — and your experience betting unders — is that books are slow to fully price bad weather, leaving totals a little inflated. UnderCast estimates a weather penalty in points and subtracts it from the market total:

Adjusted Total = Market Total − Weather Penalty

The penalty is the sum of four independent, transparent components. When it's ≥ 1.5 pts, we flag an UNDER lean. Indoor and roof-closed venues get a penalty of exactly 0.

📊 Calibrated on 15 years of results

These weights aren't guesses — they're tuned against every game 2010–2024 (nflverse closing lines + Open-Meteo weather). The backtest showed rain and 10–19 mph wind are the real, repeatable under signals, that extreme wind is already priced in, and — surprisingly — that snowy games trend OVER, so snow gets no under penalty. The tables below reflect that.

🌬️ Wind — the heavyweight

Wind kills the deep passing game and long field goals. The edge lives in the 10–19 mph range the market underrates; at 20+ mph books adjust hard, so the model flattens there instead of piling on.

🌧️ Precipitation — rain unders, snow fades

Rain is the single strongest signal in the data (~56% unders over 15 yrs, 62% recently). Snow is different: those totals are set low to begin with and historically go over, so the model treats snow as neutral rather than an under.

🌡️ Cold & Heat

Cold came out close to neutral in the backtest, so its weight is trimmed (wind, which often accompanies cold, carries the real effect). Values use the wind-chill "feels-like" temperature — see below.

🥶 Wind chill — cold × wind

Cold bites harder when it's windy. Rather than the raw thermometer reading, the cold penalty uses the "feels-like" temperature from the National Weather Service wind-chill formula — so a 30°F day at 20 mph (which feels like ~17°F) is scored as the deep-freeze game it actually plays like.

WC = 35.74 + 0.6215·T − 35.75·V^0.16 + 0.4275·T·V^0.16

T = temp °F, V = wind mph. Applied only when it's cold (≤ 50°F) and breezy (≥ 3 mph), the range the formula is defined for. Wind still carries its own separate penalty for its effect on passing and kicking — wind chill only sharpens the cold component.

📡 Where the data comes from

When deployed to a real web host, UnderCast pulls live data from two free, key-free sources — no sign-up required:

  • Schedule, venues & market totals — ESPN's public NFL scoreboard (real games, kickoff times, indoor/outdoor flags, live scores and the current over/under).
  • Weather — Open-Meteo forecast keyed to each stadium's coordinates and matched to the kickoff hour (temperature, sustained wind, gusts, precipitation).
